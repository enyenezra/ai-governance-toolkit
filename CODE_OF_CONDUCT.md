# Code of conduct

AIGovKit welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of
experience. Discuss ideas and changes respectfully. Ask before sharing other
people's information and give contributors credit for their work.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and sharing private information
without consent are unacceptable. Report concerns privately to an active
maintainer. Maintainers should investigate discreetly, allow affected people
to respond when appropriate, and apply proportionate action such as a warning,
content removal or restriction from project spaces.
